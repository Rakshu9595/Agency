Our 5-Stage Design Process

  1. 1
    Discovery & Persona Mapping: We review your brand guidelines, customer goals, competitor interfaces, and target conversions.
  2. 2
    Wireframes & User Flow: Rapid layout sketching to validate UX structure without visual distraction.
  3. 3
    High-Fidelity Visual Design: Crafting full-color screens, modern dark/light modes, iconography, and typography in Figma.
  4. 4
    Interactive Prototyping & Review: Interactive walkthrough where we collect client feedback and execute structured revisions.
  5. 5
    Asset Export & Code Handoff: Complete Figma file transfer with organized styles, SVGs, and optional production HTML/CSS.
