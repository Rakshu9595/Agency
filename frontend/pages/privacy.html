Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026. How Pravyuk collects, handles, and safeguards your project data.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project brief or create a client account at Pravyuk, we collect your name, business email, phone number (optional), project specifications, and feature requirements. Payment information is securely processed directly through Razorpay; Pravyuk never stores credit card details or bank passwords on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We process your project information exclusively to prepare custom quotations, communicate during development sprints, provide staging build access, and issue tax-compliant invoices. We do not sell, rent, or monetize client data under any circumstances.

3. Non-Disclosure & Confidentiality

All client project briefs, intellectual concepts, design assets, and database schemas are treated with strict confidentiality. We sign mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) upon client request before project initiation.

4. Contacting Us

For questions regarding your data privacy, to request data deletion, or to update your contact details, please email our security officer at hello@pravyuk.com.
