How Much Does a Business Website Cost in India? [Complete 2026 Breakdown]

Pravyuk Technical Team • Updated October 2026 • 8 Min Read

If you have ever requested website quotes from different agencies and freelancers in India, you have probably received prices ranging anywhere from ₹3,000 to ₹1,50,000+ for seemingly the same request.

Why is there such a massive discrepancy? Because "a website" can mean anything from an uncustomized WordPress template filled with bloated plugins that crashes under traffic, to a hand-crafted, high-speed custom web application engineered for search engines and high conversion rates.

In this comprehensive guide, we demystify website development costs in India, breakdown each expense category, and help you determine a realistic budget for your business.

2. What Primary Factors Drive Website Development Cost?

Website pricing is not arbitrary; it is driven by several tangible technical and design factors:

  • UI/UX Design Customization: A bespoke Figma design tailored to your specific brand identity and conversion path requires user flow research and visual prototyping, which costs more than reusing a pre-made template.
  • Number of Unique Templates: A 10-page website where every page shares the same general layout is cheaper to develop than a 5-page site with interactive pricing calculators, filterable portfolios, and custom quotation forms.
  • Backend Complexity & Integrations: A static informational site requires no database. Adding client portals, user authentication, inventory synchronization, or payment integrations (like Razorpay or Stripe) requires backend API engineering.
  • Performance & Core Web Vitals Optimization: Writing lean Vanilla HTML/CSS and fast Node.js endpoints requires skilled engineering, but yields near 100/100 Lighthouse scores that outperform bloated WordPress themes.
  • Content Readiness: If you supply approved text and high-resolution images, costs stay low. If the agency must write copywriting from scratch, pricing will reflect that effort.

3. Ongoing & Infrastructure Costs You Must Plan For

Beyond initial design and development, every live website has fundamental infrastructure requirements:

Domain Name

₹800 to ₹1,400 per year for standard .com or .in domains registered through reputable registrars like Cloudflare or Namecheap.

Web Hosting

₹0 to ₹2,500/year for static modern frontend hosting (e.g., Vercel, Netlify) or ₹500 to ₹1,500/month for dedicated Node.js / database cloud hosting.

SSL Certificate

Free (₹0) with Let's Encrypt or Cloudflare. Beware agencies that charge thousands annually for standard free SSL certificates.

Maintenance & Support

Optional ₹2,500 to ₹6,000/month retainers for uptime monitoring, cloud backups, security dependency patching, and minor content updates.

4. Red Flags: Why ₹3,000 Websites Often Cost More in the Long Run

Offers claiming "Full 10-page dynamic website with free domain and hosting for ₹2,999" are widespread. Here is what usually happens behind the curtain:

  • Zero Code Ownership: The agency retains ownership of your hosting account and code, holding your domain hostage when you request changes.
  • Security Vulnerabilities: Pirated ("nulled") WordPress themes and plugins are frequently used, leading to malware injections and Google blacklisting.
  • Terrible Mobile Performance: Sluggish load times exceeding 6 seconds lead to 50%+ bounce rates on mobile networks.
  • Hidden Handover Fees: Charging exorbitant renewal fees or refusing to provide source code files unless a ransom is paid.

5. Checklist Before Approving a Website Proposal

Ensure your development agreement explicitly covers:

  • 100% Code Ownership: You own the Git repository, assets, and design files upon completion.
  • Responsive & Accessible: Verified on iOS, Android, tablets, and desktop displays.
  • Milestone Payments: Clear payment terms (e.g., 30–50% advance, milestone releases, balance on live deployment).
  • Warranty Period: At least 30 days of post-launch bug fixing included at no extra cost.
