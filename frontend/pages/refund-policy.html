Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: October 2026. Transparent refund and dispute resolution terms for website development.

1. Zero Upfront Financial Commitment

Submitting a website brief and receiving an itemized proposal from Pravyuk is completely free. Clients incur zero financial liability until they formally accept an approved quote.

2. Advance Deposit Refunds

If a client requests project cancellation prior to the commencement of design and coding sprints (within 48 hours of advance payment), the deposit is refundable minus payment gateway transaction fees (typically 2-3%). Once custom sprint work has started, the deposit compensates for engineering hours invested.

3. Milestone Payments

Milestone payments are released only after the client inspects and approves the delivered stage on our live staging link. Once deliverables for a verified milestone have been reviewed and accepted by the client, that milestone payment is considered final.

4. Dispute Resolution & Inquiries

If you experience any dissatisfaction or technical concern regarding your deliverables, our agency leadership is committed to prompt resolution. Contact us directly at hello@pravyuk.com.
